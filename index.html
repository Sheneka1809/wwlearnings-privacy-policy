Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 27, 2026

1. Introduction

WWLearnings is an educational application designed to help college and engineering students understand technical concepts, their importance, real-world applications, and career relevance.

2. Information We Collect

WWLearnings collects the following information when users create an account:

  • Name
  • Email address
  • A custom WWLearnings User ID generated by the application

3. Account Registration

Users can register or sign in using Google Sign-In or Email and Password authentication. Firebase Authentication is used to manage account authentication.

4. How We Use Information

The collected information is used to:

  • Create and manage user accounts.
  • Identify registered users.
  • Provide access to educational content.
  • Maintain and improve the application.

5. Learning Content and Audio

WWLearnings provides educational text and audio content in English and Tamil to help students understand technical topics and explore their real-world relevance.

6. Data Security

We take reasonable measures to protect users' information from unauthorized access, alteration, or disclosure.

7. Third-Party Services

WWLearnings uses Firebase services for authentication and related application functionality. For more information, please refer to Google's Firebase Privacy and Security information:

Firebase Privacy and Security

8. Children's Privacy

WWLearnings is intended for college and engineering students. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under the age of 13.

9. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be published on this page.

Account Deletion – WWLearnings

Users can request deletion of their WWLearnings account and associated personal data using the link below.

How to request deletion

  1. Open the Account Deletion Request Form.
  2. Enter your registered email address.
  3. Submit your deletion request.

We will verify the request and manually process account deletion within 24 hours.

Data deletion

Your account information, including your name, email address and WWLearnings User ID, will be deleted along with associated personal account data.

The email address submitted through the request form will be used to verify and process your request.

After the deletion request has been successfully processed, the submitted Google Form response will also be deleted. We may retain information where required by applicable legal obligations.
